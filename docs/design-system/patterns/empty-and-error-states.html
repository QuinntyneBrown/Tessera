Pattern

Empty and error states

When a list has nothing to show, a stream cannot play or progress fails to save, the learner needs three things: what happened, what is safe, and what to do next. These states say it in the place the content would have been.

Overview

Empty and error states replace content, so they sit where the content would be and are sized to that space: a status row inside the combobox popup, a panel over the video picture, a notice above the SCORM player, or a whole page. They never appear as a toast, because a toast disappears and the problem does not.

  • Empty means the request worked and returned nothing: "No results". No danger colour, no Retry.
  • Recoverable error means trying again can work: "Learners could not be loaded. Your selections are safe." with Retry.
  • Unrecoverable error means trying again cannot work in this browser or session: "This browser can’t play this stream". No Retry; say what would help instead.
  • Blocked means the content exists but a rule hides it: "Locked until “Ask better questions” is complete." This is a reason, not an error.

Structure

Every empty or error state has the same three parts in the same order: a heading or lead sentence (what happened), a body sentence (what is safe or why), and at most one action. Icons are decorative and repeat the meaning of the text.

      Responsive behaviour

      • Status rows wrap: the message takes the first line and Retry drops below it at 320px. The message is never truncated.
      • The video error panel covers the stage; when the stage is too short for the alert at 400% zoom, the alert scrolls inside the panel rather than clipping. Below --layout-breakpoint-md the control bar sits below the picture, so the panel never competes with it.
      • Notice actions (.alert__actions) wrap under the text; with two actions the primary comes first in reading order.
      • Full empty states centre in their container up to 28rem wide; use the compact variant inside lists, cards and panels narrower than --layout-breakpoint-sm.

      Accessibility

      Role and pattern

      Errors that interrupt (the video panel, a new save failure) use role="alert". Empty results and list errors inside a combobox are announced by the combobox's own polite live region, which lives outside the popup so closing the popup never cuts an announcement short. See Understanding 4.1.3 Status Messages and Understanding 3.3.1 Error Identification.

      Keyboard

      KeyAction
      EnterIn the combobox with no active result, retries an error or loads more; the input keeps focus.
      TabReaches Retry in a video error panel or a SCORM notice; the combobox status row stays out of the Tab sequence.
      Enter, SpaceActivates Retry, Retry save or Exit without saving.
      EscCloses the combobox popup with its status row; errors in panels and notices are not dismissed by Esc.

      Focus

      Move focus only when the content under focus disappears. The video error heading (tabindex="-1") takes focus when focus was inside the player; after Retry, focus moves to Play. The SCORM save notice takes focus only when the learner tries to exit while unsaved. Closing a notice never strands focus on <body>: it returns to the visible activity heading.

      Labelling

      Each notice is a <section> named by its heading through aria-labelledby. Icons are aria-hidden. Locked items keep their reason in aria-describedby.

      Motion

      Empty and error states appear without animation. They replace a spinner or skeleton in place, so nothing slides or shakes; a shaking field is never used to signal an error.

      Touch

      Retry in a status row is small (32px) because the row is the target area around it; Retry and Retry save in panels and notices are 44px.

      Content

      Error formula

      What happened + what is safe, or why + what to do. "Learners could not be loaded. Your selections are safe." + Retry. "Progress not saved. Your latest progress is kept on this page. Try saving again."

      Empty formula

      What is empty + how to get something, only when there is a way. "No learners match “Zed”. Check the spelling or search by email." "No course loaded. Waiting for the LMS to supply a course."

      • Headings are short statements in sentence case: "Connection lost", "No access to this stream". No "Oops", no "Error:" prefix, no exclamation marks.
      • Say what is safe whenever something is: learners worry most about losing selections or progress.
      • Name the next step the learner can take ("Sign in again or ask the organiser"), not the internal cause. Technical detail (the codec string) goes in the body, after the plain sentence.
      • Offer Retry only when retrying can work. Never offer Retry for an unsupported browser.
      • Use a curly apostrophe and quotes in copy (can’t, “Zed”). See content and tone for the full library.
