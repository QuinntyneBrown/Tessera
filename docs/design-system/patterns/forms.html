Pattern

Forms

Forms let administrators assign learners, set due dates and leave notes. One column, labels above fields, errors only after the person has had a chance to answer, and one clear action at the end.

Overview

A form is a sequence of form fields that ends in one action. The rules on this page sit above the individual components: where labels go, when errors appear, how the person learns what went wrong, what the submit button does while it waits, and how destructive changes are confirmed.

  • Layout: one column, at most 40rem wide (.form). Short related fields may share a row from 768px up (.form__row--2).
  • Labels sit above their field, always visible. Placeholders show an example format, never the label.
  • Validation runs when focus leaves a field, then again on every change once an error is showing. A required field stays quiet until the person leaves it or submits, exactly as the combobox mock does.
  • Submit stays enabled. Pressing it validates everything, moves focus to the problem, and only then sends.

Structure

Course context, then fields in the order the person thinks about them, then actions. The combobox's reading order is label, field, list, error, hint; every other field follows the same order: label, control, error, hint.

Layout rules

  • Spacing: --space-6 (24px) between fields, --space-2 (8px) from label to control and from control to hint. Groups of related fields sit in a .fieldset with a <legend>.
  • Label placement: above the control, left-aligned, at every width. Side-by-side labels break at 200% text and in narrow columns, and they separate the label from the error.
  • Width: fields fill the form column; the field width does not hint at the expected length, the hint does.
  • Required and optional: when most fields are required, say so once at the top and mark the rest "(optional)". Required fields carry required or aria-required="true". An asterisk alone is never the only signal.
  • Actions: .form__actions ends the form, right-aligned with the primary last. One primary per form. Cancel or Reset is secondary and never the default action.

Validation timing

MomentWhat happens
Page load, first typingNothing. An untouched field never shows an error, even if it is empty and required.
Focus leaves the field (blur)The field is marked touched and validated. For the combobox, "leaves" means focus moves outside the whole component (input, chips, list), not between its parts.
Input while an error is showingRe-validate on every change. The error disappears the moment the value is valid; a new error replaces the old text in place.
SubmitEvery field is marked touched and validated. One error: focus moves to that field. Two or more: the error summary appears above the form and takes focus.
Server rejects the requestField-level problems are shown as field errors and listed in the summary; a whole-form failure ("Learners could not be assigned") is a danger alert above the actions with Retry.

    Destructive submits (remove learners, delete an assignment) get a confirmation step that names the object and the consequence.         Responsive behaviour

        • The form is one column at every width. .form__row--2 pairs short fields only from --layout-breakpoint-md (768px); below it they stack.
        • Below 768px .form__actions stacks full width in column-reverse, so the primary (last in DOM) sits on top and Cancel below it.
        • At 320px and at 400% zoom, labels, hints and errors wrap; nothing truncates except chip labels, which show the full name in a tooltip. Many chips scroll inside the field after --combobox-chip-list-max-height (8rem) so the input stays reachable.
        • The combobox list is bounded by the visual viewport; when the on-screen keyboard leaves little room it anchors to the input row and may cover chips, never the input.
        • With text enlarged to 200% or WCAG text-spacing applied, fields grow taller; their minimum height is 44px, never a fixed height.
        • Long forms on phones: keep the error summary at the top of the form, not in a fixed bar, so it scrolls with the content and does not cover fields.

        Accessibility

        Role and pattern

        Native <form>, <label for>, <fieldset>/<legend> and native inputs. The learner picker follows the APG Combobox pattern. Errors meet 3.3.1 Error Identification and 3.3.3 Error Suggestion; destructive confirmation meets 3.3.4 Error Prevention. Do not ask for information already given (3.3.7 Redundant Entry).

        Keyboard

        KeyAction
        Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields and actions in DOM order. The combobox is one stop (its toggle and chip remove buttons are tabindex="-1"); Tab closes its list.
        EnterIn a single-line field, submits the form as the browser normally does. In the combobox with the list open, toggles the active learner instead.
        Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to the field and scrolls it into view with its label visible.
        EscIn the combobox: closes the list, then clears the query. Never resets the form.
        SpaceToggles checkboxes and switches; activates buttons.

        Focus

        On submit with one error, focus the invalid field; with more, focus the error summary. After a successful submit that keeps the form, focus stays on the submit button. In the confirmation dialog, focus starts on Cancel and returns to the trigger when it closes. Focus is never moved on blur validation; the person keeps their place.

        Labelling

        Every control has a visible <label>. Hints and errors are tied with aria-describedby, error first. Invalid fields set aria-invalid="true". Required fields set required or aria-required="true". Success and progress text goes to one polite role="status" region per form; the combobox has its own live region outside its popup.

        Motion

        Errors appear without animation. Border colour changes over --duration-fast; the submit spinner keeps turning under reduced motion at 2 s per revolution because it reports status. Scrolling to an error uses scroll-behavior: auto when reduced motion is requested.

        Touch

        Fields and buttons are 44px tall. Use the right type and autocomplete so phones show the right keyboard (type="date", type="email"). The "Clear all selections" text button meets the 24px minimum and sits apart from the field so it is not hit by accident.

        Content

        • Labels are short nouns in sentence case: "Learners", "Due date", "Note to learners". No colons, no "Please".
        • Hints say why or how, in one sentence: "Learners see this on their dashboard." Put format examples in the hint, not only in the placeholder.
        • Errors say what to do, specific to the field: "Select at least one learner.", "Enter a due date after today, such as 30 October 2026." Never "Invalid input" or "This field is required".
        • Error summary heading gives the count and the goal: "Fix 2 problems to assign learners".
        • Submit names the outcome: "Assign learners", "Preview assignment", "Save changes". Never "Submit" or "OK".
        • Confirmation asks a question with the count ("Remove 3 learners?"), states the consequence and what is kept, and repeats the verb on the danger button.
        • Failure follows the error formula: what happened, what is safe, what to do: "Learners could not be assigned. Your selections are safe. Check your connection and try again." See content and tone.

        Do and don't

        Sources

        Composed from these mocks:

        • combobox: the "Assign learners" form for Workplace safety essentials; required learners field marked touched on focus-out of the whole component and on submit; error below the field and before the hint; "Clear all selections" in the hint row; submit focuses the input when nothing is selected; "Preview assignment" primary and its role="status" confirmation.
        • scorm-player: autosave status ("Last saved at", "Not saved"), "Progress not saved" notice with "Retry save", and "Exit without saving" in the same notice instead of a dialog.

        The due-date and note fields and the destructive dialog are derived: not present in the mocks; they follow the system rules.

        Drift fixed during extraction:

        • Combobox field min-height 40px beside 44px buttons; both are --control-height-md (44px).
        • Two disabled treatments (light border vs dashed); unified on the dashed edge with readable --color-fg-disabled.
        • Focus outline 3px with 3px offset on the page vs 2px on the field; one 3px ring with --focus-ring-offset 2px everywhere.
        • Hint and error sizes 0.8125rem and 0.85rem; snapped to --font-size-sm.
        • The mock marks the required field with an aria-hidden asterisk only; the pattern states the rule once at the top and marks optional fields in words, keeping aria-required.
