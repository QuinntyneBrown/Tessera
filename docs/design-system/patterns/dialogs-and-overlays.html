Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Anything that floats above the page interrupts it. Pick the lightest layer that does the job: inline first, then a popup tied to its trigger, and a modal dialog only when the learner must decide before going on.

Overview

Tessera has five overlay kinds. They differ in whether they block the page, where they sit in the DOM, and where focus goes.

  • Dialog (modal): blocks the page, traps focus, returns it to the trigger. For decisions and short focused tasks.
  • Drawer / sheet: a dialog anchored to an edge. Below 640px every dialog becomes a bottom sheet.
  • Popover (menu, account panel): non-modal, light dismiss, tied to a trigger button.
  • Inline popup (combobox listbox): non-modal, focus stays in the input, rendered directly after its field in the DOM.
  • Tooltip: non-interactive supplementary text on hover and focus; never the only place information lives.

Before any of these, ask whether the content can be inline. The SCORM player's unsaved-exit warning is inline: it reuses the save-error notice and moves focus to its heading instead of opening a dialog.

The combobox decision (ADR-0001)

The combobox list is a CDK overlay rendered as a native popover with the inline location: the panel's host is inserted directly after the field, inside the component, yet it still enters the browser's top layer, so no ancestor overflow clips it and it stacks above dialogs. The reading order is label, field, list, error, hint. This is what lets VoiceOver on iOS reach the options by swiping on from the input, which a panel appended to the end of <body> cannot do (Safari has no aria-owns). The mock models it with a fixed-position popup placed right after the field. The live region stays outside the popup. Without the Popover API, the panel falls back to a container inside the nearest open native dialog, or the shared overlay container.

  • Busy: while the confirmed action runs, the dialog sets aria-busy="true", the confirm button shows its spinner, Cancel is disabled, and the dialog stays open. On failure it shows an inline .field__error-style message in the body; on success it closes and a toast confirms.
  • Inline confirmation (SCORM "Exit course" while unsaved): the save-error notice opens with "Retry save" and "Exit without saving", its heading takes focus, and its alert text is not replayed when the notice reopens.
  • Nested: a menu or combobox inside a dialog opens above it (top layer, or --z-popover in fallback). Never open a dialog from a dialog; replace the content or go to a page.

Responsive behaviour

  • Mobile sheets: below --layout-breakpoint-sm (640px) .dialog becomes a full-width bottom sheet with --radius-xl top corners, at most 90dvh tall; the footer is sticky, buttons share the row, and the safe-area inset is respected.
  • Dialog width is min(--dialog-width, 100vw − 32px); the body scrolls, never the page behind it. At 400% zoom the header and footer stay reachable because the sheet body scrolls.
  • Combobox popup: at most min(24rem, 60dvh), bounded by the visual viewport. When status plus a visible option cannot fit around the full field, the popup anchors to the input row, keeping the input visible (it may cover chips).
  • Menus cap at min(20rem, 100vw − 32px) and flip above the trigger near the bottom edge.
  • Tooltips cap at min(24rem, 100vw − 32px); on touch they appear on long-press or focus and never hold required content.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Dialogs follow the APG modal dialog pattern using native <dialog>. The combobox follows the combobox pattern (focus stays in the input; aria-activedescendant). Menus follow the menu button pattern; the outline toggle follows the disclosure pattern; tooltips follow the tooltip pattern and meet 1.4.13 Content on hover or focus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EscCloses the topmost layer only: tooltip first, then a popup, then a dialog. In the combobox: dismiss tooltip, then close the list, then clear the query.
Tab / Shift+TabDialog: cycles within the dialog. Combobox and menus: close the popup and continue in the page order.
Enter, SpaceOpens a dialog, menu or disclosure from its trigger; activates the focused dialog button.
↓ / ↑Menu and combobox: move through items without wrapping (combobox) or with wrapping (menu).

Focus

  • Into: a dialog focuses its least destructive action (Cancel) or, for a form, the first field. An inline notice focuses its heading (tabindex="-1").
  • Within: showModal() makes the rest of the page inert; nothing behind is reachable by Tab or a screen reader's virtual cursor.
  • Return: on close, focus returns to the trigger. If the trigger is gone (the removed learner's row), focus moves to the next row, else the previous one, else the list heading. Never to <body>.
  • Popups never take focus from the combobox input; the active option is shown with a 2px accent outline, and the field keeps the amber ring.

Labelling

Dialogs set aria-labelledby to the title and aria-describedby to the consequence text, so both are read on open. Menus are named by their trigger. The combobox listbox is named "Learners"; the popup's status row is outside the Tab sequence and its messages are announced by the host's live region, which sits outside the popup.

Motion

Dialogs and sheets fade and rise over --duration-slow with --ease-enter; exits use --ease-exit. Popups appear instantly. Under prefers-reduced-motion the durations are zeroed.

Touch

Dialog buttons and the close button are 44px; on sheets the buttons fill the row. Tapping the backdrop closes non-destructive dialogs only; a destructive confirmation closes by Cancel, Close or Esc, never by a stray tap.

Content

  • Dialog titles ask the question or name the task: "Remove 3 learners?", "Change due date". Not "Are you sure?" or "Warning".
  • The body states the consequence and what is kept: "Their progress is kept if you assign them again."
  • The confirm button repeats the verb and object: "Remove 3 learners"; the escape is "Cancel". Never "OK" / "Yes" / "No".
  • Menu items that open a dialog end with an ellipsis: "Remove learner…".
  • Tooltips are a few words, no period, no links: the full chip label, or "Show outline" for an icon button.

Extracted from these mocks and decisions:

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Popup and menu radius 6px (combobox); normalised to --radius-md.
  • Combobox motion duration hard-coded to 0ms in light; overlay motion now comes from --duration-*, zeroed only by reduced motion.
  • No z-index scale in the mocks; stacking now uses --z-dropdown to --z-tooltip.
  • Field 40px vs 44px buttons; the field driving the popup is --control-height-md.
