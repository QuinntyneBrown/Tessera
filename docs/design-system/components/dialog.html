Dialog

A modal dialog stops the page to ask one question: remove these learners, change this due date, leave without saving. Everything behind it is inert until the person answers or dismisses it.

Overview

Use a dialog when the person must decide or supply something before carrying on, and the decision belongs to the current page: confirming a destructive action, a short form ("Change due date"), or a warning that blocks progress. Examples on this page are drawn statically inside canvases; in a product the dialog opens with showModal() over a backdrop.

  • Default: a heading, one or two sentences, and the actions.
  • Destructive: a danger icon, a heading that names the loss, and a .btn--danger confirm.
  • Form: up to about four fields; longer forms deserve their own page.
  • Scrollable: long content (terms, an activity's details) scrolls inside the body while header and footer stay put.
  • Mobile sheet: below 640px every dialog becomes a bottom sheet with a sticky footer.

Do not use a dialog for status that needs no answer (use a toast or alert), for errors inside a component such as the video player (its error panel stays inside the player), for a list of actions (use a menu), or for content people want to read alongside the page. See dialogs and overlays.

Anatomy

  1. Title.dialog__header h2: --text-h3. It names the dialog (aria-labelledby) and asks the question.
  2. Close buttonGhost icon button, aria-label="Close"; same as Cancel. Omitted only when the dialog must be answered.
  3. Body.dialog__body: 24px inline padding, scrolls when content is long; aria-describedby target for short dialogs.
  4. Footer.dialog__footer: actions end-aligned, safe action first, primary or danger last; 12px apart; wraps.
  5. Surface and backdrop--color-bg-surface-raised, --radius-lg, --shadow-4, --z-modal; the backdrop is --color-bg-backdrop at --z-overlay.

Sizes

Width is set by --dialog-width and never exceeds the viewport minus 16px on each side. Pick the smallest size the content fits.

Small · 24rem

Confirmations and destructive questions.

Medium · 32rem (default)

Short forms and explanations.

Large · 48rem

Two-column forms or a preview, such as an assignment preview listing the selected learners.

.dialog--sm 24rem, default 32rem, .dialog--lg 48rem; all capped at 100vw − 2 × --space-4.

Busy · invalid · failed.
  • Busy: aria-busy="true" on the dialog dims the body; the submit button shows its spinner; fields go read-only and Cancel becomes aria-disabled. Esc and the backdrop do nothing until the request ends. Focus stays on the submit button.
  • Invalid: the dialog stays open, the field gets aria-invalid="true" and an error linked with aria-describedby, and focus moves to the first invalid field. The error names the fix.
  • Failed: the request failed; the dialog stays open with a role="alert" alert at the top of the body saying what happened and that nothing changed, and the confirm becomes "Try again". This is the video player's error panel rule: say what went wrong, offer one recovery.
  • Open and closed: closed dialogs are not in the DOM's accessibility tree (<dialog> without open); opening sets the page behind inert.

Responsive behaviour

  • ≥ 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm): centred, width from the size token, max height 100dvh − 2 × --space-8; the body scrolls when content is taller.
  • < 640px: bottom sheet. Full width, --radius-xl top corners, max 90dvh, sticky footer whose buttons share the row equally and clear env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • At 400% zoom (320px CSS) the sheet rule applies, so the header, scrolling body and footer all stay reachable; the footer wraps buttons onto two rows rather than shrinking them.
  • Never put two-column layouts in small or medium dialogs; large dialogs drop to one column below --layout-breakpoint-md.

Theming

The dialog reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --shadow-4 and --radius-lg; the backdrop reads --color-bg-backdrop; width comes from the component token --dialog-width. The destructive icon uses --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-icon.

/* A host needs a wider default dialog */
.host-admin .dialog { --dialog-width: 40rem; }

In the dark theme the surface lifts to --palette-sage-850 above a sage-950 page, the backdrop deepens to 70% black, and the shadow darkens; the lighter surface carries the elevation. In the shipped packages these are --t-colorNeutralBackground1 and --t-shadow4; see theming.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal) pattern, built on the native <dialog> element opened with showModal(), which supplies role="dialog", modality, the top layer and inertness of the page behind. Labelled by its title (aria-labelledby); short dialogs add aria-describedby for the body text. Destructive confirmations may use role="alertdialog".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the dialog's controls and wraps; focus cannot leave while it is open.
EscCloses the dialog as Cancel would, and returns focus. Ignored while busy. A tooltip or open select inside the dialog takes Esc first, as the combobox tooltip does.
EnterIn a form dialog, submits from a single-line field. Never auto-confirms a destructive dialog.
Space, Enter on buttonsActivates the focused button.
Arrow keysScroll a focused scrollable body.

Focus

  • On open: the first field in a form dialog; the safe action ("Keep learners") in a destructive one; the title (tabindex="-1") when the body is long or scrollable, so reading starts at the top. This mirrors the video player's error panel, whose heading takes focus.
  • On close: back to the control that opened it. If that control no longer exists (its learner was removed), focus moves to the next logical element, such as the next row's actions or the list heading, never to <body>, as the SCORM mock's region-closing rule requires.
  • The ring is the shared amber ring; on the raised surface it still passes 3:1.

Labelling

The title is the accessible name and asks the question in words ("Remove 3 learners from this course?"). The close button is "Close". Busy dialogs keep their name; the result is announced by the page's live region ("3 learners removed") after the dialog closes. Failed requests are announced by the role="alert" inside the body.

Motion

The dialog may fade and rise 8px over --duration-slow (320 ms) with --ease-enter, and the sheet slides up from the bottom; both exit faster with --ease-exit. Reduced motion zeroes the durations, so it appears in place.

Touch

Buttons are 44px; on the sheet they share the footer row equally, so each target is wide. Tapping the backdrop closes non-destructive dialogs only; a destructive dialog ignores backdrop taps so a stray tap never confirms or loses the question.

Content

  • Title: a question or a direct statement in sentence case, naming the object and count: "Remove 3 learners from this course?", "Change due date".
  • Body: one or two sentences on the consequence ("Their progress will be deleted. You cannot undo this.").
  • Buttons repeat the verb: "Remove 3 learners" / "Keep learners". Never "Yes" / "No" or "OK" / "Cancel" for destructive choices.
  • Failure messages say what happened, that nothing changed, and what to do: "The LMS did not respond. Nothing was changed. Try again."
  • Validation errors name the field and the fix: "Choose a date after today, 9 October 2026."

Sources

Derived: not present in the mocks; follows the system rules. The nearest evidence:

  • video-player: the error panel (role="alert", heading that takes focus with tabindex="-1", one sentence, one Retry, no Retry when retrying cannot help) sets the failed-state and focus-on-title rules.
  • scorm-player: "Progress not saved" with Retry save and "Exit without saving" is the model destructive choice; closing a region never strands focus on <body>.
  • combobox: Escape is consumed by the innermost layer first (tooltip, then popup, then query), the rule for Esc inside a dialog.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • The video error stripe was 5px; any stripe inside a dialog uses --border-width-stripe (4px).
  • The error panel heading was 1.1rem and its button radius 7px; dialogs use --text-h3 (18px) and --radius-md.
  • The video error panel's dark-theme button label used --page-canvas; dialogs use --color-fg-on-accent and --color-fg-on-danger.
