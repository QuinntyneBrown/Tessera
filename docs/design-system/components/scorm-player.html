SCORM player

The SCORM player runs a course package inside the LMS: a course header with live status, a lesson outline, the course content in a sandboxed frame, and navigation that always says why a step is unavailable.

Overview

Use the SCORM player wherever a learner takes a packaged course ("Better conversations at work", four activities). It owns loading, the outline, navigation, saving and exit; the course content inside the frame is the author's and keeps its own look.

  • Use it for SCORM 1.2 packages supplied by the LMS (an extracted manifest or a ZIP). SCORM 2004 packages are recognised and refused with a clear error until their rules are supported.
  • Don't use it for live sessions (use the video player) or for plain lesson pages the LMS renders itself; those use ordinary page layout with a stepper.
  • It composes system parts: stepper for the outline, alert for notices, description list (inline) for status, buttons for navigation, and the wrapping sidebar layout from layout grid.

Anatomy

  1. Course title.page-header__title: the page's main heading, --font-size-3xl on phones and --font-size-4xl from 48rem, tight letter spacing.
  2. Inline status.dl.dl--inline: "Completion" and, once a save has happened, "Save status". Terms bold, values muted; pairs wrap.
  3. Notice.alert above the layout: loading (with Cancel), saved, course complete, no course loaded, or a danger notice for a failed save. 4px status stripe.
  4. Outline panel.outline-panel, a <nav> labelled by its "Course outline" heading, with .outline-panel__head and the toggle (below 48rem only).
  5. Stepper.stepper of .stepper__item buttons: marker (number or check), title, and a detail line ("Current activity · Not reported", "Complete", or the lock reason).
  6. Content bar.content-player__bar: "Activity 1 of 4" and the lesson heading, which takes focus on every lesson change.
  7. Content frame.content-player__frame: the sandboxed <iframe> titled "Course content: {lesson}", --content-player-height tall (460px default).
  8. Actions row.content-player__actions: Previous, Exit course and Continue (primary). Unavailable ones are aria-disabled, never removed.
  9. Reason.content-player__reason: why a navigation action is unavailable, linked with aria-describedby; end-aligned, start-aligned below 48rem.

Not visible: one polite role="status" live region per player for "Lesson complete", "Progress saved", "Course exited" and similar messages.

The player has one layout; these are its states, matching the mock's state controls and the shipped component. The content frames hold a short plain lesson text; real course content keeps its own styles.

Sizes

One size. Width comes from the layout: the outline takes a 15.5rem basis and the player keeps at least 30rem before the outline wraps above it. The content frame's height is a token, --content-player-height (default 28.75rem / 460px, from the mock), because a frame cannot size itself to cross-origin content. Set it on the player or an ancestor to suit the LMS page; courses scroll inside the frame when they are taller.

States

The interactive parts are outline items, the navigation buttons and the outline toggle. Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state; products get them from :hover, :focus-visible, :active, aria-current, aria-disabled, aria-expanded and data-complete.

  • Current (aria-current="step"): accent-subtle fill, 4px accent stripe on the leading edge and an accent title. It is never shown by colour alone: the detail line says "Current activity".
  • Complete (data-complete="true"): the marker fills with the accent and shows a check; the detail line says "Complete".
  • Locked (aria-disabled="true"): readable --color-fg-disabled text, dashed marker ring, no hover; it stays focusable and its detail line gives the reason.
  • Continue / Previous unavailable: aria-disabled="true" with a dashed edge and aria-describedby pointing at the reason text. Activation does nothing.
  • Loading: the player sets aria-busy="true" while the package loads; outline and frame show skeletons.

Both themes

The player chrome follows the page theme through semantic tokens. The course content inside the frame does not: it keeps the author's styles in both themes (see Theming).

Responsive

  • Wrapping layout (.sidebar-layout): the outline (basis 15.5rem) sits beside the player while the player can keep 30rem; otherwise it wraps above it. Because the rule is content-based, not viewport-based, 200% text or 400% zoom never squeezes the frame.
  • Below 48rem (--layout-breakpoint-md): the outline collapses behind a "Show outline" / "Hide outline" toggle (aria-expanded, aria-controls); choosing a lesson collapses it again. Bar, actions and outline head use tighter padding (12 × 16px), and the reason text aligns to the start.
  • Widening past 48rem hides the toggle; if it had focus, focus moves to the current outline item instead of being lost.
  • Actions wrap rather than shrink; at 320px each button takes its own line and the reason sits below them.
  • Course header: the title and status wrap; long course titles break anywhere rather than overflow (overflow-wrap: anywhere).
  • The content inside the frame is the author's responsibility; the player gives it the full width of the column and the token height.

Theming

Player chrome reads semantic tokens only: surfaces and lines (--color-bg-surface, --color-border-default), ink (--color-fg-default, --color-fg-muted), accent for the current and complete steps, danger for the save error, and the shared focus ring. In the shipped package the same decisions are the shared @tessera/theme properties (--t-<tokenName>); defaults follow the system light/dark preference, and an explicit theme on the document root, a container or the player host takes precedence (see theming).

Course content keeps its own styles. The frame is cross-origin and sandboxed; the player cannot and does not theme it. A course that sets its own background keeps it in both themes; a course that sets none is transparent, so the frame's --color-bg-surface shows through while the course's own text colour stays as authored. That can leave dark default text on a dark surface, so ask authors to set a background. Do not inject theme CSS into course content.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The outline is a <nav> labelled "Course outline" containing an ordered list of buttons; the current one has aria-current="step". Each item's name is its title (aria-labelledby) and its state is its description (aria-describedby). Navigation buttons follow the APG Button pattern; the narrow-screen outline toggle follows the APG Disclosure pattern. Notices are labelled sections; a failed save uses role="alert"; status messages go to one polite role="status" region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through notice actions, the outline toggle, outline items, the frame's content, Previous, Exit course and Continue, in DOM order. Locked items and unavailable buttons stay in the sequence.
Enter / SpaceOn an outline item: opens that lesson (ignored when locked). On Previous / Continue: moves one lesson. On the toggle: shows or hides the outline.
EscInside the expanded outline on narrow screens: collapses it and returns focus to the toggle.
Keys inside the frameBelong to the course content; the player never intercepts them.

Focus

On every lesson change (outline, Previous, Continue) focus moves to the lesson heading (tabindex="-1"), so screen readers announce the new lesson and the next Tab enters its content. Loading, the exit warning and "No course loaded" move focus to their notice heading when focus was inside the player; a closing notice never strands focus on <body>. The shared ring (3px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) marks focus.

Labelling

  • The <iframe> has a title that names the lesson: "Course content: Start with listening", updated on every change.
  • Unavailable actions use aria-disabled="true" plus aria-describedby to the reason text ("Continue is unavailable: locked until “Give useful feedback” is complete.").
  • Markers are aria-hidden; the detail line carries the state in words.
  • Live region messages: "Start with listening complete.", "Course complete.", "Progress saved.", "Course exited.", "Course exited without saving." An unchanged status is never re-announced, and messages from one task are joined so none is overwritten unread.

Motion

Only colour transitions (--duration-fast) on hover, and the skeleton shimmer and spinner while loading. Under prefers-reduced-motion transitions are zeroed and the shimmer stops; the spinner slows to 2 s per turn. Lesson changes never animate. Motion inside the course is the author's.

Forced colours

The current outline item gets a 2px Highlight outline, buttons a ButtonText border, and the spinner system colours, so state survives without author colours.

Touch

Outline items are full-width rows at least 44px tall; navigation buttons and the toggle are 44px (--control-height-md). Hover fills apply only where the pointer can hover.

Content

  • Position reads "Activity 2 of 4"; lesson and course titles come from the package and are rendered as text only.
  • Navigation labels are "Previous", "Continue" and "Exit course". Arrows are icons, not characters in the label.
  • Reasons name the action and the rule: "Previous is unavailable: this is the first activity.", "Continue is unavailable: locked until “Give useful feedback” is complete." Never "Disabled" or a lock icon alone.
  • Status values are plain words: Completion "Unknown" / "Complete"; Save status "Last saved at 10:42" / "Not saved".
  • Notices pair a short heading with what happens next: "Progress not saved — Your latest progress is kept on this page. Try saving again." Loading says what it is doing: "Checking the course package…".
  • Outline detail lines: "Current activity", "Complete", "Not reported" (SCORM's own word for no status yet), joined with " · ".

Code

In an Angular app, use the shipped component; the LMS supplies the package, the attempt and the host callbacks (loadAttempt, saveState, prepareDelivery). Content is delivered from an origin different from the LMS's.

<tsr-scorm-player
  [source]="source"
  [attempt]="attempt"
  [host]="host"
  [limits]="limits"
  (event)="onEvent($event)"
/>

  • Font sizes 0.76, 0.8, 0.85, 0.87, 0.9 and 0.91rem snapped to --font-size-xs / --font-size-sm; the 1.15rem lesson heading is --font-size-lg; weight 750 is 700.
  • Button radius 7px is --radius-md; the 12px panels are --radius-lg.
  • Off-grid padding (0.55, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.2, 1.3rem) snapped to the 4px scale.

  • Breakpoint max-width: 47.5em is --layout-breakpoint-md (48rem); the 1440px shell is --layout-container-max.
  • Unavailable buttons used a light solid border; they use the system's dashed edge with readable text.

  • .sr-only used clip; it is .visually-hidden with clip-path: inset(50%).
