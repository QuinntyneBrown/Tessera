Try the interaction

  • Space or K pauses and resumes. Resume jumps back to live.
  • M mutes. ↑ ↓ change volume by 5.
  • C toggles captions. F toggles fullscreen; Esc leaves it.
  • Controls hide after 3 s while playing and never while a control has focus.
  • The LIVE badge becomes a “Go to live” button when playback falls behind.
