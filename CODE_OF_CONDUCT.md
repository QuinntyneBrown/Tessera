# Code of Conduct

Tessera is a community for building accessible software. Participation should
be respectful and free from harassment, regardless of background, identity,
disability, experience, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and consideration.
- Discuss ideas and implementation choices on their merits.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Respect different experiences, communication styles, and accessibility needs.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve their effects.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in a review.
- Other behavior that makes participation unsafe or exclusionary.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
documentation, and other project spaces. It also applies when a participant
represents Tessera in a public setting.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to
[quinntynebrown@gmail.com](mailto:quinntynebrown@gmail.com), using the subject
`[Tessera conduct] <brief description>`.

Include the relevant context, links or records, and any immediate safety
concerns. Share only the personal information necessary to review the incident.
Reports are not appropriate material for public issue threads.

If a concern involves the maintainer or there is an immediate platform safety
issue, GitHub's
[abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
provides an independent reporting route for conduct on GitHub.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews reports and determines an appropriate response.
Responses can include a private warning, removal of content, limits on
participation, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

The response takes account of the conduct, its impact, and any repeated
behavior. The maintainer explains moderation decisions to affected participants
where practical and protects reporter privacy. Confidentiality may be limited
when disclosure is necessary for safety or platform enforcement.

Participants may request reconsideration through the same private reporting
channel. Retaliation is itself a violation of this policy.
